Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is an addendum to our normal Privacy Policy, and covers additional data that may be collected by the Imbue Studio product, especially during this early beta phase.

In short: you agree to share product data from your Imbue-hosted workspaces with Imbue to help improve Imbue Studio.

On the Explorer plan, we explicitly export data from your remote workspaces. We do our best to redact and remove sensitive data, but such methods are always and necessarily incomplete.

On the Limited plan, we try to avoid collecting any data from inside your workspaces — our goal is to make your data private and secure. However, there will almost certainly be bugs or extenuating circumstances during the beta period that could expose data in those workspaces.

If you are particularly concerned about privacy, we recommend that you use the Imbue Studio app without any Imbue account at all, and disable network access and/or disable the error reporting functionality by building directly from the source code on GitHub (during this beta period, error reporting is turned on by default. We hope to make it easier to turn off at some point in the not-too-distant future).

See our full privacy policy here: https://imbue.com/privacy
