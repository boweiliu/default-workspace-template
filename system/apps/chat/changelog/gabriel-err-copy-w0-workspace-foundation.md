Chat failures without a reason of their own use the workspace's shared fallback sentences instead of "request failed (HTTP 503)", "could not reach the workspace", "unknown error", "The chat list could not open a new chat (503)" or "The chat app answered HTTP 500.".

- A transcript, side conversation or model list that could not be loaded says "The workspace didn't answer. Try again in a moment."; a change that got no answer says Imbue Studio can't tell whether it went through.

- Creating a chat, the empty chat list's new chat, reading or applying a message sent from the launcher, and the secret card's submit and decline now read failures through the shared reader.
