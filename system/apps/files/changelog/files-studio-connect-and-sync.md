Files says how each synced folder stands as Imbue Studio reports it.

- A synced folder's state comes from Imbue Studio on the computer instead of a guess made by comparing names on both sides. While it syncs, the circular arrows on its icon turn, and its tooltip reads "Syncing with Kims-Mac" over how far along it is ("25% done"); once it settles it reads "Up to date with Kims-Mac"; a sync that stopped shows the orange alert, its tooltip ("Sync with Kims-Mac stopped" over Studio's reason) and the folder itself say why, and the folder points to retrying it in Imbue Studio; a sync switched off reads "Not syncing with Kims-Mac". A copy whose computer is asleep, or whose Imbue Studio is closed, reads "Kims-Mac isn't answering" rather than a guess.

- An empty synced folder that is syncing says so, and how far along it is, in place of "Empty".

- The sidebar keeps up with Imbue Studio while a Files window is on screen: it checks every few seconds while a folder is syncing and every 15 seconds otherwise, and right away when the window comes to the front. However many Files windows are open, your computers are asked at most once every couple of seconds, and not at all while no Files window is on screen. A computer that is asleep, or whose Imbue Studio is closed, shows its shares as not answering instead of the whole list failing.
